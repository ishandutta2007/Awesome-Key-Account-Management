# Awesome-Key-Account-Management

## Top Key Account Management Tools Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Strategic Account Planning, Relationship Mapping & Revenue Expansion*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Key Account Management (KAM) Tools**. These tools help enterprise sales and account management teams map buying committees, plan account strategies, track relationship strength, and identify expansion opportunities — turning key accounts into long-term revenue engines.



**Examples** include Salesforce Account Management, DemandFarm, Kapta, Revegy, Gainsight, Planhat, Totango, Altify, Membrain, and ChurnZero (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom account planning workflows, and transparent customer data — ideal for teams prioritizing data sovereignty and developers building account intelligence platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[CXM-Tool](https://github.com/antigravitysoham-eng/CXM-Tool)**  

  Full-stack Customer Experience Management platform built with React, Vite, Express, and SQLite. Features interactive dashboard with retention/churn metrics, customer directory, health checks, contract lifecycle management, onboarding tracker, customer training hub, and AI co-pilot for real-time database intelligence .



- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  Open-source CRM fork of SugarCRM with modules for sales, marketing, and support. Extensible for key account management with custom modules for relationship mapping, account planning, and opportunity tracking. AGPL-3.0 licensed.



- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  

  Open-source CRM with customizable entities, workflow automation, and email integration. Can be configured for account management with custom fields, relationship tracking, and reporting. GPL-3.0 licensed.



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Modern open-source CRM with contacts, companies, and opportunities management. Growing ecosystem of extensions and integrations suitable for building KAM features. AGPL-3.0 licensed.



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERP with CRM, project management, and support modules. Comprehensive platform for managing customer accounts, contracts, and relationships. GPL-3.0 licensed.



- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source ERP/CRM with sales, CRM, project, and contract management modules. Suitable for key account management with customization. LGPL-3.0 licensed.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)** — Open-source Laravel CRM with lead management, pipeline tracking, and email integration. MIT licensed.

- **[Corteza](https://github.com/cortezaproject/corteza)** — Open-source low-code platform with CRM, workflow automation, and role-based access. Apache 2.0 licensed.

- **Relationship Mapping Tools** — NetworkX, Cytoscape.js, and Sigma.js for building custom relationship visualizations.

- **Customer Success Libraries** — Open-source health scoring and churn prediction frameworks.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CXM-Tool** for account health and lifecycle management, **SuiteCRM** or **EspoCRM** for CRM data, **NetworkX** for relationship graph analysis, and **Metabase + PostgreSQL** for account analytics to build a complete self-hosted KAM stack.
